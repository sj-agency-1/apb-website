# Finding Your 'Diamonds'

The AI service will conduct a deep audit and find your hidden potential.

## What are 'Diamonds'?

These are your unique skills, experience, and knowledge that can be monetized. We often don't notice our own strengths because they seem ordinary to us.

## How the Audit Works

AI analyzes your answers to specially developed questions and identifies patterns that indicate your unique potential.

## Why This Matters

Without understanding your 'diamonds', it's impossible to create unique positioning and stand out from competitors.
