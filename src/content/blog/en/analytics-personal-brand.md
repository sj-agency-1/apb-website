# Analytics for Personal Brand

Which metrics to track and how to interpret them?

## Key Indicators

- **Reach** — how many people see your content
- **Engagement** — how the audience interacts with content
- **Conversion** — how many people take target actions

## Analytics Tools

Use built-in social network analytics, Google Analytics, and specialized services for tracking mentions.
