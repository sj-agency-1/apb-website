# Business Confidentiality

Your business model is a trade secret. How do you protect it when working with external partners?

## NDA — Your First Defender

A Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) must be signed before any collaboration begins.

## What to Include in an NDA

- Definition of confidential information
- Duration of obligations
- Liability for breach
- Exceptions to confidentiality
