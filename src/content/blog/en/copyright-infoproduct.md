# Copyright for Info Products

You spent time creating a perfect, structured product. Now you need to protect it!

## Why Content Protection is Important

In the digital age, copying content has become easier than ever. Your unique developments can be stolen and used by competitors.

## Protection Methods

1. **Copyright registration** — official confirmation of your authorship
2. **Depositing** — recording the date of material creation
3. **Client agreements** — legal restriction of distribution
