# Professional Logo Brief is Your Legal Protection

Your Technical Specification is not only an instruction for the designer but also a legal basis.

## Why You Need a Detailed Brief

A well-composed technical specification protects you from:

- Misunderstanding with the designer
- Ownership disputes
- Unexpected revisions for additional fees

## Key Brief Elements

1. Target audience description
2. References and anti-references
3. Color palette and fonts
4. File format requirements
