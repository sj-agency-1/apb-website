# Secrets of Effective PR

How to attract media attention to your personal brand?

## Creating Newsworthy Stories

Regularly generate interesting news about your activities that will interest journalists.

## Media Contact Database

Compile a database of journalists who write on your topic and build long-term relationships with them.

## Expert Comments

Offer yourself as an expert for comments — it's a great way to get media mentions.
