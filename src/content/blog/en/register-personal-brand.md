# How to Register a Personal Brand: 3 Steps for Legal Protection

Your name is a trademark. But what exactly should you protect? The 'Architect of Personal Brands' service at Stage 2 creates such unique positioning that it becomes your main legal asset.

## Step 1: Determine What to Protect

Before contacting a lawyer, you need to clearly understand which elements of your brand are subject to protection:

- Your name or alias
- Logo and visual elements
- Slogan and key messages
- Methodologies and proprietary developments

## Step 2: Check for Uniqueness

Use trademark databases to verify the uniqueness of your brand. This will help avoid conflicts with existing brands.

## Step 3: Submit a Registration Application

After identifying elements and verifying uniqueness, prepare documents for trademark registration with the appropriate authorities.
