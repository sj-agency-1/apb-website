# Trademark in Digital

Your digital address is part of your asset. How do you protect it legally?

## Domain as an Asset

Your domain name should match your trademark and be registered in your name as an individual or legal entity.

## Social Networks

Reserve your username across all key social networks, even if you don't plan to use them yet.

## International Protection

If you plan to work in international markets, consider registering your trademark in multiple jurisdictions.
